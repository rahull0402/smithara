Doctor profile

Dr. Sushmitha

Founder & Chief Dentist · Smithara Dental Care

Dr. Sushmitha is the founder and chief dentist at Smithara Dental Care, Bangalore. She completed her BDS and went on to pursue a Fellowship in Restorative Clinical Dentistry from AIAD, certified by Open Medaversity, Hong Kong, along with advanced fellowships in Aesthetic Dentistry and Laser Dentistry.

With over 7 years of hands-on experience, she has treated 5,000+ patients, focusing on minimally invasive and painless care — whether it's a child's first dental visit or a complete smile makeover.
